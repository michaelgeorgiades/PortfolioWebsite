About G5 Properties

We are a professional house flipping business dedicated to revitalising properties across the Redlands region of Australia.

At G5 Properties, we see potential where others see problems. Whether it's a neglected family home, a fire-damaged property, or a house that's simply seen better days, our team has the expertise and vision to transform it into a beautiful, market-ready home.

With years of experience in property renovation, we pride ourselves on quality workmanship, attention to detail, and delivering exceptional results that benefit both sellers and future homeowners.

Our Mission

G5 Properties, based in Capalaba and serving Brisbane-wide, transforms houses into lasting homes through practical, cost-effective solutions that put end-user value first. Our skilled, integrity-driven trades deliver consistent, high-quality outcomes through ongoing training and trusted partnerships with buyers and sellers. From construction to transaction, we operate with respect and reliability—ensuring every project stands the test of time.

Meet the Team

Meet Chris Georgiades and Heather, the driving force behind our home renovation passion. Chris, a seasoned builder with over 10 years leading residential and commercial construction teams, pairs perfectly with Heather, a registered nurse who's honed her community focus and eye for detail.

Our Journey

We kicked off by renovating our own home, sparking an unstoppable drive to breathe new life into properties. After studying the local Brisbane market, we launched our business — looking for rundown older homes and transforming them into standout gems.

What Sets Us Apart

Blending Chris's building expertise with Heather's meticulous touch, we target the neighbourhood's most neglected houses, uplifting entire streets. Our hands-on approach ensures every project exceeds expectations, turning eyesores into enduring value for buyers and communities.
